## What the two stages must not share

- **Audience.** Vehicle owners, DIY maintenance people and practical gift buyers are not parents of
  3–6-year-olds. Do not run one blended voice at both. Say plainly where a single brand account
  helps and where it hurts, and give each stage its own boards, keywords and calls to action.
- **Keywords.** The Pip keyword set is picture-book and bedtime intent. The logbook set is
  vehicle-maintenance and gift intent. They must not be mixed in one pin title or board.
- **Conversion goal.** Stage 1 has a real, purchasable destination. Stage 2 has no destination that
  can take money — its conversion is awareness, saves, follows and email/interest capture, and it
  must never carry a "buy" call to action.
